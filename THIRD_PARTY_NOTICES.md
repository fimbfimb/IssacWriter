## bilingual_book_maker

This application's **Translate** tab drives the external tool
[bilingual_book_maker](https://github.com/yihong0618/bilingual_book_maker)
as a separate subprocess for bilingual book translation.

It is cloned locally into `tools/bilingual_book_maker/` (not vendored into this
repository) and runs from its own virtual environment.
